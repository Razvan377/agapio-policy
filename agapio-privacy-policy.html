Agapio

Privacy Policy · Effective September 18, 2026

Agapio is a reading app. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, and what we don't do with it. If a sentence here doesn't answer your question, the contact section at the bottom will.

01

What we collect

Agapio works fully without an account — browsing, reading, and keeping stories all work offline, on your device, with nothing sent to us. The table below covers what's collected once you use a feature that needs it.

DataWhenWhy
Email address Only if you create an account (email, Google, or Apple sign-in) To identify your account and let you sign in again
Reading activity Only if signed in Categories you've chosen, stories you've kept or passed, and your reading streak — synced so your progress follows you across devices
Usage analytics Always, anonymously unless signed in A count of which stories are opened and which categories they belong to, so we know what's actually being read. Not linked to you unless you're signed in, and even then it's a one-way log we use for aggregate counts — not something we build a profile from
Subscription status Only if you subscribe Apple and RevenueCat handle the purchase and tell us whether it's active — we never see your card details or Apple ID
Display preferences Always, on-device only Theme (light/dark) and reading style — stored only on your device, never sent to us at all
02

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad network embedded in the app.
  • No selling or renting your data to anyone, for any reason.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No location data collected.
03

Who else touches your data

We use a small number of specialist services to run Agapio. Each only sees what it needs to do its one job:

  • Supabase — stores account and reading-progress data, and handles sign-in. Their privacy policy.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all App Store purchases. Their privacy policy.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google. Their privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — verifies subscription status with Apple on our behalf. Their privacy policy.
04

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

Signed-in account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Agapio's You tab, under Account — this permanently removes your account and everything synced to it. Anything that only ever lived on your device is entirely in your control; uninstalling the app removes it.

05

Children's privacy

Agapio isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

06

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date above. We won't quietly widen what we collect without saying so here first.

Questions about this policy or your data — write to rey.rey.agapi@gmail.com.
